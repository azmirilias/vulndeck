Azmir Ilias

Author and owner of VulnDeck. I’m based in Malaysia and work across cybersecurity, community building, collaboration and education.

Why I built VulnDeck

I created and maintain VulnDeck to make public vulnerability and threat-intelligence data easier to explore and use. It brings exploitation signals, advisories and open reference data into one lightweight platform for security and DevSecOps work.

For me, useful work brings people, knowledge and opportunity together. VulnDeck is one way I share practical security information and make it easier for others to put it to use.

My work

My wider work includes cybersecurity capability development, community initiatives, collaboration and sharing experience with the next generation. One example is JURUS, a competency pathway built around demonstrable, hands-on cybersecurity ability.

VulnDeck is my independent project. I author and own the platform; its data sources remain credited and subject to their publishers’ terms. See Sources and project provenance.

Ownership and source

The VulnDeck source code is released under the MIT licence. Its public-data connectors do not make it affiliated with the publishers or projects whose data it references.
